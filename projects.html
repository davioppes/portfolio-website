A Short Climb - The Brainless Game Jam Submission

My submission for The Brainless Game Jam, where I spent 6 days working as a solo developer to create a puzzle platformer game! I am really proud of this submission, as I have gotten a lot of great impressions of the game from others, and I managed to do everything on my own in the time I had.

This was a very rewarding experience for me as I learned about many new skills that are required for video game development, including level and puzzle design, tilesets, pixel art, and godot program structure.
Play the game on the browser!
